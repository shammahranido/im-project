About Us

Vision

Cebu City is the only City that provides effective and sustainable veterinary health services which guarantee quality, safe fishery and food animal meat products.

Mission

Commitment to serve Cebu City consumers and animal owners as the vanguard of animal and public health through strict and sustained safe meat and fishery products, comprehensive animal control, promotion of animal welfare and veterinary medicine as basic services.

Core Values

We extend animal and public health service in the most honest, resilient, ingenious and sincere way possible.

Goals

  • To sustain as one of the most organized and relevant veterinary offices in the country.
  • To employ in all aspects of our work the demands of modern food inspection system, animal control, disease prevention and promotion of animal welfare of international standards.
  • To maintain the dignity of our office and its servants by providing quality and prompt service.
  • To provide hygienic and sanitary environment to our workers and clients.

Services Offered

ANIMAL HEALTH

A. Cebu City Animal Birth/Population Control Program

  • Conduct Spay and Neuter
  • Conduct Impounding of Stray Dogs
  • Conduct Anti-Rabies Vaccination
  • Promote Pet Adoption
  • Perform Humane Euthanasia
  • Conduct Information Education Campaign on Rabies Protection and Animal Bite Prevention

B. Livestock Disease Prevention and Control Program

  • Livestock Disease Management, Treatment, Prevention and Control
  • Equine Infections Anemia Surveillance
  • Close Monitoring/Livestock Disease Surveillance
  • Avian Influenza (Bird Flu) Surveillance
  • Foot nd Mouth Disease Surveillance
  • African Swine Fever Surveillance
  • Caprine Arthritis Encephalitis Surveillance

PUBLIC HEALTH

  • Conduct of Meat Inspection (Ante-Mortem and Post-Mortem) in Different Private Abattoirs/Dressing Plant
  • Registration and Licensing (Private Abattoir/Meat Establishment/Meat Handlers/Butchers/Helpers, etc.)
  • Conduct of Re-Inspection on Meat from Different Meat Stakeholders outside Cebu City
  • Conduct Post-Abattoir Inspection in Different Supermarkets in Cebu City
  • Conduct Post Abattoir Inspection in Different Public Markets in Cebu City
  • Meat Regulation Enforcement and Hot Meat Operation

FISHERIES

  • Conduct of Fish and Marine Products Inspection in Different Public/Private Markets and Supermarkets
  • Monitoring and Enforcement of Laws Related to the Protection Marine Products in different public/private Market and Supermarket
